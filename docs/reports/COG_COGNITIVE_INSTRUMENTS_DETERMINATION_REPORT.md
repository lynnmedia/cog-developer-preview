# Cognitive Instruments Architecture & Governance Determination Report

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Determination**: `COG_COGNITIVE_INSTRUMENT_MODEL_VALIDATED`
- **Scope Evaluated**: HDCPs 040–045 (Instrument Contract, Drift Lens, Epistemic Map, Provenance Lineage Instrument, Tension Field, Composed Observability Proof).
- **Core Finding**: Observational Cognitive Instruments project, reveal, and map complex cognitive phenomena across all Cog architectural layers (routines, domain capabilities, memory, tools, and CogOP) without mutating, adjudicating, or inventing cognition.

---

## 2. Synthesis of Empirical Instrument Evidence

### 2.1 Observational Contract Discipline (HDCP-040)
- Established `CognitiveInstrument`, `InstrumentFinding`, and `InstrumentObservationReport` across 6 projection types (`graph`, `tabular`, `hierarchical`, `vector_field`, `timeline`, `epistemic_matrix`).
- Enforced strict distinction: Routines transform cognition; Instruments observe and reveal cognition without acting as hidden decision engines.

### 2.2 Longitudinal Drift Observability (HDCP-041)
- `Drift Lens` exposed assumption dropping, grounding shifts, emergent tensions, and confidence trajectories across multi-month revisions without forcing judgment on whether drift was beneficial or detrimental.

### 2.3 Epistemic State Topography (HDCP-042)
- `Epistemic Map` categorized artifacts across 11 discrete epistemic roles, exposing hidden staleness and regulatory contradictions without compressing epistemic state into a single scalar confidence score.

### 2.4 Cross-Runtime Provenance & Lineage (HDCP-043)
- `Provenance & Lineage Instrument` reconstructed end-to-end transformation graphs and mutation timelines spanning source anchors, tool evidence, domain capabilities, and CogOP multi-hop exchanges.

### 2.5 Unresolved Tension Field Topology (HDCP-044)
- `Tension Field` mapped competing positions and downstream microservice exposure while preserving tension insolubility.

### 2.6 Composed Multi-Angle Observability (HDCP-045)
- Applied all four instruments to a realistic multi-stage robotics lifecycle, proving complete observational coverage without mutating underlying state.

---

## 3. Governance and Next-Layer Gate Decision

- **Determination**: `COG_COGNITIVE_INSTRUMENT_MODEL_VALIDATED`
- **Gate Scope**: This determination ratifies Cognitive Instruments as a permanent Cog architectural layer and permits consideration of the network layer.
- **Boundaries Preserved**: No additional instruments, Big Net, or distributed network platforms are authorized without explicit task scope.
