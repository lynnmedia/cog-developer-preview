## 3. Governance and Admission Evidence

1. **Admission Threshold Ratification**:
   - Formally updated from 2 domains to `minimum_materially_distinct_domains: 3` and `minimum_deterministic_proof_cases: 3`.
   - Verified that two-domain candidates fail admission.
2. **Anti-Absorption & Boundary Discipline**:
   - CogLib strictly imports zero domain libraries, machine editions, persistence drivers, or application CLI/UI shells.
   - Duplicate routines (e.g. standalone `VALIDATE`) are rejected by the Admission Standard (`REJECT` disposition), preventing library bloat.
3. **Lossless Composition Invariants**:
   - Proven that `pipeline`, `branch`, `merge`, and `guarded_transition` preserve provenance lineage monotonically and aggregate unresolved tensions without silent drop.

## 4. Expansion Guidance

- The determination `COGLIB_STANDARD_ROUTINE_MODEL_VALIDATED` confirms that the foundational standard library model is sound.
- This determination **permits consideration** of future candidate routines under the 3-domain admission governance standard, but **does not authorize automatic implementation**.
- Any future candidate routine must follow the same rigorous lifecycle:
  $$\text{Semantic Definition} \to \text{CognitiveRoutine Implementation} \to \text{3-Domain Proof Harness Validation} \to \text{Admission Standard Evaluation}$$
